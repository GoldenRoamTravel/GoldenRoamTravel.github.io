The heart behind Golden Roam

Meet Suzanne

Hi, I'm Suzanne, an independent travel agent, part-time NHS practice nurse and full-time travel lover!

I created Golden Roam Travel because I believe life doesn't stop after 50 – in fact, some of the best adventures are still ahead of us! My love of travel has taken me across Asia and Africa, on European city breaks and to luxurious Caribbean escapes. From boutique hotels and hidden gems to once-in-a-lifetime luxury experiences, I love discovering places that make you feel truly alive.

Whether you're dreaming of a romantic escape as a couple, planning an unforgettable girls' adventure, craving a little luxury or ticking off that bucket-list destination, I'm here to help make it happen.

As your independent travel agent, I offer a personal, friendly service, taking the stress out of planning and creating tailor-made trips to suit your interests, style and budget. From finding those special hotels to creating incredible itineraries, I'll help you turn your travel dreams into reality.

Because life is for living, exploring and making memories – at every age.
